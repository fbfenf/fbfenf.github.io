> It becomes yours once your mouth remembers it. Three to five sentences a day, fifteen minutes.

## 1. Listen to the whole first

Listen to today's three to five sentences three times in a row without looking at the text. The play button at the bottom of the script screen reads on from the sentence you chose. Just catch what is going on and how it is said.

## 2. Learn one sentence at a time

1. Tap a sentence to open it. Its translation and buttons appear.
2. If something is unclear, check the words and the grammar in **Explain**.
3. Listen to that one sentence with the speaker next to it, and repeat it about ten times, until you can say it at the same speed.
4. In **Practice**, use Speak to see how much of it you got right. Dictation, Recall and Blanks let you go over it once more.
5. When it comes out naturally, tap **Done** and move on to the next sentence.

## 3. Say it all together

Say the three to five sentences in a row without looking. Open again only the ones you get stuck on.

A sentence you marked Done comes back in the review on the **Today** tab about when you would forget it. Do only what is due that day.

## Today's 15 minutes

| Time | What | In the app |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 2 min | Listen to the whole | The play button at the bottom, three times |
| 8 min | One sentence at a time | Listen and repeat with the speaker, Explain, Practice |
| 5 min | Say it all together | Three to five sentences without the text |
| Next day | Review | Only the sentences on the Today tab |

## If you want to do more

Ten sentences in one go is too many: you stop paying attention to the later ones. Stack them five at a time.

1. Finish the first set (sentences 1 to 5).
2. Take a short break, then do the second set (6 to 10) the same way.
3. At the end, say both sets in a row.
