> Download free sample scripts below to experience potential of iScript right away!
>
> **How to Use:**
> 1. Click a **download button** below to download the `.xlsx` or `.zip` file.
> 2. Open **iScript App** → Tap **Library** tab → Tap **+ Add Script**.
> 3. Select the downloaded file and start learning!
>

## 💡 Make Your Own Script

Want to create your own script? All you need is an Excel file!

| Column A (Target Lang) | Column B (Your Lang) |
|:---|:---|
| 안녕하세요! 반가워요. | Hello! Nice to meet you. |
| 이거 얼마예요? | How much is this? |
| 맛있게 드세요. | Enjoy your meal. |

*   **Tip**: Put the language you want to learn (e.g., Korean) in Column A, and your native language (e.g., English) in Column B.
*   **Save**: Make sure to save as **Excel Workbook (.xlsx)**.
