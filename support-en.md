faq:
  - q: "Is iScript free? Can I remove the ads?"
    a: "Every feature is free. The app is supported by ads, and there is nothing to buy to remove them. People who paid for the app or for Pro before it became free still see no ads."
  - q: "The AI explanation doesn't appear."
    a: "Please check, in this order:\n\n1. Your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, runs iOS 27 or later, and has it turned on in iPhone Settings\n2. You are connected to the internet\n3. If you have used up the day's allowance, the app tells you when you can use it again\n\nOn a device that doesn't support it, connecting a [Gemini key](/api-en/) gives you sentence explanations."
  - q: "What works on an iPhone that can't use the AI features?"
    a: "Listening, the four kinds of practice (Speak, Dictation, Recall, Blanks), review and the word list all work. Some built-in scripts come with explanations that need no AI. With a Gemini key you also get explanations for the scripts you import. Example sentences, questions and Talk with AI work only on a device that supports the iPhone's AI."
  - q: "Do I need a Gemini key? Does it cost anything?"
    a: "No. It is an option for people whose device can't use the iPhone's AI and who want sentence explanations. You get the key from Google AI Studio, and any charges follow Google's terms. [AI setup](/api-en/) shows how."
  - q: "What can I import?"
    a: "Paste text you copied, or import a file: Excel (xlsx), CSV, plain text and subtitles (srt). Text and subtitles are split into sentences, and missing translations can be filled in while importing. There are also [sample scripts](/resources-en/) to try right away."
  - q: "Can I start without a script of my own?"
    a: "Yes. The app comes with ten built-in scripts (travel, café, work and more). Choose one from + in the Library and start studying."
  - q: "Where are my speaking practice recordings kept?"
    a: "A recording stays on this iPhone for a moment and is deleted when you close practice. Turning speech into text is done by the iPhone; for a language the iPhone doesn't have, the audio is sent to Apple's speech recognition service. Nothing reaches the developer."
  - q: "How does the word list work? Can I use Anki?"
    a: "Tap the star next to a word in an explanation and it is saved to the Words tab. Saved words can be reviewed on their own, and Export in the word list makes a file that Anki can open."
  - q: "How do I back up, or move to another device?"
    a: "Use “Create a backup file” in Settings and keep the file somewhere like iCloud Drive. On the new device, choose that file with “Restore from a backup”. There is no automatic sync between devices."
  - q: "My study records are gone."
    a: "iScript has no server and stores everything on the device, so deleting the app deletes your scripts and records. If you made a backup file earlier, “Restore from a backup” in Settings brings them back. Without a backup file they can't be recovered."
  - q: "I can't hear anything."
    a: "iScript plays sound even with the silent switch on. Check the volume, and that the iPhone isn't connected to Bluetooth earphones somewhere else. If there is still no sound, close the app completely and open it again."
  - q: "Does it work on iPad? Offline?"
    a: "It works on both iPhone and iPad. Listening (with the device voice), practice and review work offline. AI explanations and Talk with AI need the internet."
